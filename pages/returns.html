ART. 01

How to return

1

Contact us within 14 days

Email ipordiseparfums@gmail.com or reach us on WhatsApp with your order number and reason.

2

Get your return label

Our team will send you a return label and instructions within 24 hours of your request.

3

Pack & send the item

Place the item in its original packaging, sealed and unused. Drop it off at the designated courier point.

4

Refund processed

Once received and inspected, your refund is processed within 3–5 business days.

ART. 02

Return conditions

  • Return requests must be submitted within 14 days of the delivery date.
  • Items must be completely unopened, unused, and returned in original packaging with seals intact.
  • Opened perfumes, personalised orders, and gift sets cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

ART. 03

Refund methods

  • Cash refund — For cash-on-delivery orders, refund is sent via bank transfer within 3–5 business days.
  • Store credit — Opt for IPORDISE store credit and receive a 10% bonus on top of your refund amount.

ART. 04

Exchange policy

Changed your mind on the scent? You can exchange for a different fragrance instead of a refund. Same conditions apply — item must be sealed and unopened. No price difference charged for items of equal or lesser value.

ART. 05

Packing tips

  • Use the original box and packaging materials.
  • Seal the parcel securely with tape.
  • Add bubble wrap or padding inside for protection.
  • Attach the return label visibly on the outside.

ART. 06

Contact & support

Our support team is available Mon–Sat, 9am–8pm. Reach us at ipordiseparfums@gmail.com.
