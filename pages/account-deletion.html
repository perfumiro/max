Privacy & account control

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your IPORDISE account and associated personal data inside the app, or request deletion by contacting our privacy team.

From the IPORDISE app

  1. Sign in and open Account.
  2. Choose Privacy & Security.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Confirm your email address and current password.
  5. Review the permanent-deletion warning and confirm. The app deletes the account immediately after successful verification.

Request deletion without the app

Email the address below from the email connected to your IPORDISE account. Include only your account email and the words “Account deletion request”. Never send your password or payment details.

Email a deletion request

What happens next

In-app deletion is processed immediately after password verification. Email requests require identity verification by the privacy team. Account profile data, saved addresses, wishlist, cart, profile photo and other account-linked data are deleted. Order records may be retained only where required for legal, fraud-prevention or accounting obligations and are unlinked and anonymized, as described in the Privacy Policy.
