## Description

Discover fragrance with IPORDISE, a curated perfume shop for customers across Morocco.

Browse designer, niche, Arabian, women’s, men’s, and unisex collections. Explore product details and available sizes, save favourites, build your bag, and place a cash-on-delivery order in Moroccan dirhams.

With an optional customer account, you can keep your profile and addresses up to date, synchronize your favourites and bag, review your orders, and manage privacy and notification preferences. You can also contact IPORDISE Care from the app for order help or product guidance.

Features include:

- A searchable fragrance catalogue with product and size details
- Favourites and a quantity-aware shopping bag
- Cash-on-delivery checkout for physical products
- Customer profiles, saved addresses, and order history
- Private customer-care conversations
- English, French, and Arabic support

Availability, delivery coverage, and product pricing are confirmed during checkout.
