## Full description

IPORDISE brings its curated fragrance boutique to your phone. Explore designer, niche, and Arabian fragrances, compare available sizes and live prices, and discover products by brand, audience, collection, and scent family.

Build a wishlist, keep a shopping bag across sessions, and place cash-on-delivery orders for delivery across Morocco. Signed-in customers can save delivery addresses, review order history and shipping updates, manage account preferences, contact IPORDISE Care, and request an account data export or deletion.

Key features:

- Curated fragrance catalogue with live availability and pricing
- Product details, galleries, scent notes, sizes, and recommendations
- Search, brand discovery, collections, favourites, and shopping bag
- Server-validated checkout with duplicate-order protection
- Cash on delivery and customer order tracking
- Customer accounts, saved addresses, order history, and support conversations
- English, French, and Arabic interface with right-to-left support

IPORDISE currently serves customers in Morocco. Product availability, delivery coverage, and payment methods are confirmed in the app before an order is submitted.

## App access instructions

Browsing, search, product pages, the bag, guest checkout, order tracking, and Help are available without a customer account. Account-only features include saved addresses, synced wishlist/cart, order history, profile settings, data export, and account deletion.

For review, either let the reviewer create and verify an account using an email they control, or provide an active dedicated reviewer account in Play Console. Never place review credentials in this repository or the public listing.
